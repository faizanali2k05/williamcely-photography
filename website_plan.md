# Website Design Plan: William Cely Photography

## 1. Overview
A professional, elegant, and responsive portfolio website for William Cely Photography, inspired by Carlota Nurse Photography.

## 2. Brand Identity
- **Name:** William Cely Photography
- **Focus:** Maternity, Birthdays, Couples, Quinceañeras, Weddings.
- **Vibe:** Elegant, Timeless, Professional.
- **Location:** Grand Prairie / Dallas-Fort Worth, TX.

## 3. Color Palette
- **Primary:** #FFFFFF (White) - Background
- **Secondary:** #F9F7F2 (Off-white/Cream) - Section backgrounds
- **Accent:** #D4AF37 (Gold) or #B8860B (Dark Goldenrod) - Buttons, links, highlights
- **Text:** #333333 (Dark Charcoal) - Main text
- **Text Muted:** #777777 (Grey) - Subtitles

## 4. Typography
- **Headings:** 'Playfair Display', serif (Classic and elegant)
- **Body:** 'Montserrat' or 'Lato', sans-serif (Modern and readable)

## 5. Site Structure (Single Page with Smooth Scroll or Multi-page?)
*Decision: Single page for simplicity and modern feel, with dedicated sections.*

- **Navigation Bar:** Logo, Home, Portfolio, About, Contact.
- **Hero Section:** Full-screen background image with tagline "Capturing Your Most Precious Moments".
- **Services/Categories:** Grid of 5-6 categories (Maternity, Weddings, etc.) with hover effects.
- **About William:** Introduction text and a professional photo.
- **Featured Gallery:** A masonry or grid layout of best works.
- **Testimonials (Optional):** Quotes from happy clients.
- **Contact Section:** Lead capture form and contact details (Phone, Instagram link).
- **Footer:** Social icons and copyright.

## 6. Technical Stack
- **HTML5**
- **CSS3 (Custom styles + Bootstrap 5)**
- **Bootstrap 5 (Grid, Navbar, Components)**
- **JavaScript (AOS for animations, Smooth scroll, Form handling)**
- **Google Fonts**
- **FontAwesome (Icons)**
